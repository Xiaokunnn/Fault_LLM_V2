     Insulation                                                                   
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
     Pumps supplied by DESMI can in general be insulated for heat or cold except at the top of the pump housing – i.e. up
     to the base flange on the motor bracket as shown on the diagram below. No holes or screen openings on the motor
     bracket can be covered by insulation.                                        
                                                                                  
     An electric motor will become hotter if mounted on a fully insulated pump. The ball bearings and/or coils in the motor
     can overheat if a pump is insulated all the way up to the motor flange.      
                                                                                  
     Unrestricted ventilation of the motor bracket is therefore necessary to provide sufficient cooling of pump and motor
     bearings – in pumps with bearings (A02 version) and without (A12 version).
