    1.02 Principle of Operation                        
    Flexible Impeller pumps are shaft driven. During shaft rotation
    the pump creates a partial vacuum by the increase of suction cell
    volume, which allows atmospheric pressure to push additional
    fluid into the suction cell. This fluid is carried around the outside of
    the Impeller to the discharge side where it is discharged from the
    cell by a reduction in volume caused by the eccentric cam located
    in the body.
