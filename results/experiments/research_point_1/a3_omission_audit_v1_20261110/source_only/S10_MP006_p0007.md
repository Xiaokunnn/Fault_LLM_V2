      Allowable         vibration       levels     on                             
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
      DESMI       centrifugal         pumps        and    motors                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
      As standard DESMI centrifugal pumps with electric motor generate less than 2.8 mm/s vibration velocity (measured
      in 3 directions at motor flange level on vertical pumps) during a factory test.
                                                                                  
      The pump and electric motor industry generally agrees that vibration levels above 7 mm/s are damaging – i.e. this
      will result in shorter lifetimes of e.g. bearings and/or shaft seals in pumps (and bearings in motors) than normally
      expected. This agrees with the recommendations in the international standard ISO 10816-3.
                                                                                  
      But in pump installations on ships external excited vibrations from main engine and/or auxiliary machinery are
      transferred from ship hull to the pump often resulting in much higher vibration velocities than 7 mm/s.
                                                                                  
      DESMI recommends the following values for protective settings:
