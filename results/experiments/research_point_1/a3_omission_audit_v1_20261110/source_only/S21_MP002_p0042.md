            5.1.5 Report Exceptions                                               
                 The Owner is to advise the attending Surveyor of all machinery for which maintenance is not
                 indicated, is incomplete, or when additional monitoring is needed for machinery with vibration
                 readings above those in the approved baseline. If either of the above mentioned situations occurs,
                 the condition of the machinery is to be to the satisfaction of the attending Surveyor.
